Technical Leadership & Experience

Wrightwood Consulting is led by an experienced construction professional and engineer with more than 15 years' experience across construction, engineering and the delivery of complex, technically demanding projects. That background sits behind every piece of advice Wrightwood gives and every project it coordinates.

Rather than entering building safety consultancy from a purely advisory or compliance background, Wrightwood's technical leadership comes from the construction industry itself — time spent on technically challenging construction and refurbishment schemes, working alongside design teams, contractors and specialist subcontractors, and seeing projects through from early design to practical completion. That's a different starting point from a desk-based compliance review, and it shapes how Wrightwood approaches every instruction: as something that has to actually get built, not just signed off on paper.

  • Over 15 years' experience in construction, engineering and complex project delivery, across both new-build and refurbishment work.
  • Extensive experience on technically challenging construction and refurbishment projects, including buildings with constrained access, occupied residents, and complex existing structures.
  • Significant practical experience navigating the Building Safety Act and the Building Safety Regulator's approval processes, gained by working through them directly rather than advising from the outside.
  • Specialist knowledge of external wall remediation, façade construction, fire safety coordination and higher-risk buildings, built up across multiple live projects rather than a single engagement.
  • A practical understanding of construction delivery, regulatory compliance and the real challenges of progressing a project through the Gateway approval process — including the kind of delays, information gaps and coordination problems that only become visible once you're inside a live submission.

Direct Experience of Gateway 2 Approval

Gateway 2 is the hardest practical obstacle most higher-risk building projects face. It's a hard stop: the Building Safety Regulator will not allow construction to begin until it is satisfied that the design, the building's fire and structural safety, and the competence of those responsible for delivering it, have all been properly demonstrated. Plenty of consultancies can explain what Gateway 2 requires in theory. Fewer have actually sat inside a live submission, responded to BSR queries under time pressure, and got a project through to approval.

2Gateway 2 approvals our technical lead has been directly involved in securing

Wrightwood's technical lead has been directly involved in securing Gateway 2 approval on two higher-risk building projects — work that involved coordinating the technical documentation, design information, consultant submissions and regulatory responses required to satisfy the Building Safety Regulator. That included:

  • Pulling together and quality-checking the technical documentation a Gateway 2 submission depends on, so it reflects the design and construction approach actually being taken.
  • Coordinating design information across architects, structural engineers, fire engineers and other specialist consultants so the submission tells one consistent story, rather than a set of documents that don't quite agree with each other.
  • Managing the practical business of getting consultant submissions finalised, reviewed and issued to a timetable, which is very often where Gateway 2 programmes actually slip.
  • Preparing and coordinating responses to the Building Safety Regulator's queries and requests for further information — the stage where many submissions stall if no one is driving it.

What this means for your project

This isn't generic Building Safety Act advice. It's experience of what a Gateway 2 submission actually looks like from the inside — where the real risks to programme sit, what the Building Safety Regulator typically pushes back on, and how to keep a multidisciplinary team's information moving in the same direction. If your project is heading towards Gateway 2, or is already inside the process and has stalled, that's precisely the kind of problem this experience is suited to.

How Wrightwood Works

Wrightwood works exclusively client-side — for the building owner, RTM company, managing agent or housing provider, not for a contractor or design firm. We appoint and coordinate suitably qualified, independent fire engineers, facade consultants and other specialists on our clients' behalf; we don't carry out FRAEW, PAS 9980 or other technical assessments ourselves. That split matters: it means our advice is shaped by what's actually needed to get a project through to completion, not by a commercial interest in any one piece of the work.

Our focus is London and the South East, with reach across England where a project needs it. If you're dealing with a Gateway 2 submission, a stalled Building Safety Regulator approval, or an external wall or remediation project that needs someone who has actually been through this process before, that's the conversation we're best placed to have.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Wrightwood's experience and how we work.

01

What does "Gateway 2 experience" actually mean in practice?

It means having coordinated the technical documentation, design information, consultant submissions and Building Safety Regulator correspondence a real Gateway 2 application depends on — not just advising on the Building Safety Act in general terms. Wrightwood's technical lead has been directly involved in securing Gateway 2 approval on two higher-risk building projects, and brings that first-hand understanding of where submissions typically stall to every project we support.

02

Does Wrightwood carry out FRAEW, PAS 9980 or fire safety assessments directly?

No. Wrightwood appoints and coordinates suitably qualified, independent fire engineers and facade consultants to carry out PAS 9980 and FRAEW assessments, and uses the findings to inform the wider project. See our guide to how a FRAEW works and what it costs for more on this.

03

Do you only work on higher-risk buildings (18m+ or 7+ storeys)?

No. While Gateway 2 and Building Safety Regulator approval only apply to higher-risk buildings, Wrightwood also supports external wall remediation and funding applications on lower-rise buildings, including the Cladding Safety Scheme route now open to some buildings under 11 metres.

04

Do you work outside London and the South East?

London and the South East are our primary focus, but we work with clients across England where a project calls for it. Get in touch with details of your building and location and we'll let you know whether we're a good fit.
