What Client-Side Project Management Actually Means

"Client-side" means we work for you — the building owner, RTM company, RMC or managing agent — rather than for a contractor or a design firm with their own commercial interest in the job. Our role is to represent your interests throughout the project: appointing and managing the design team, running the procurement process, administering the contract once a contractor is appointed, and controlling cost and programme on your behalf.

It's a different relationship from simply hiring a contractor directly, or instructing a fire engineer for a single report. Client-side project management sits above all of the individual appointments, making sure they add up to one coherent project rather than a collection of separate instructions you're trying to manage yourself.

Why a Single Point of Accountability Matters

Most building owners and boards who end up running a remediation project themselves aren't short on commitment — they're short on the specific experience of coordinating a multi-specialist construction project, which is a different skill from managing a building day-to-day. Without a single party holding the whole picture, it's easy for gaps to open up between specialists: a design assumption the contractor wasn't told about, a funding deadline nobody was tracking against the design programme, a cost increase nobody flagged until it had already happened.

Client-side project management exists to close those gaps — one team tracking the whole project, so decisions in one area are made with the rest of the project in view, and so you have a single point of contact who can answer "where are we, and what happens next?" at any point.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about client-side cladding remediation project management.

01

What does "client-side" project management mean?

It means the project manager works exclusively for you, the building owner or Responsible Entity, rather than for a contractor or design firm with their own commercial stake in the outcome — representing your interests throughout design, procurement and construction.

02

How is this different from hiring a main contractor directly?

A main contractor is responsible for delivering the construction works to the agreed design and contract. A client-side project manager sits above that relationship, appointing the contractor in the first place, administering the contract, and protecting your interests on cost, programme and quality throughout — rather than you relying solely on the contractor to manage itself.

03

Do you carry out the FRAEW or PAS 9980 assessment yourselves?

No. We appoint and coordinate a suitably qualified, independent fire engineer or facade consultant to carry out the assessment, and use the findings to inform the wider project. See our guide to PAS 9980 for more on the assessment itself.

04

How involved do we need to be day-to-day?

As little or as much as you want. We manage the day-to-day coordination and bring key decisions to you at the right points, rather than needing the board or agent involved in every individual specialist conversation.

05

What size of project do you manage?

We work across buildings of different heights and scales, from single blocks under 11 metres to higher-risk buildings requiring Building Safety Regulator submissions. Get in touch with your building's details and we'll give you an honest view of fit.
