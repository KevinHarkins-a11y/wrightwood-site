Cladding Safety Scheme 2026

Cladding Safety Scheme Funding for Buildings Under 11 Metres

Limited 8-week application window — applications opened 17 August 2026.

If your building is under 11 metres and has cladding or external wall concerns, this guide explains the new under 11m cladding funding route — who qualifies, how to apply, and what to do before the window closes.

New Funding for Buildings Under 11 Metres

New government funding is now available through the Cladding Safety Scheme for eligible multi-occupied residential buildings under 11 metres in England.

Applications opened on 17 August 2026 and there is a limited 8-week application window.

The funding is administered by Homes England through the existing Cladding Safety Scheme and is intended to address serious life-safety fire risks associated with unsafe cladding and external wall systems.

The 8-Week Application Window

Applications for buildings under 11 metres opened on 17 August 2026 and close at the end of the 8-week cladding funding window.

Responsible Entities who believe their building may have an unsafe external wall or cladding system should therefore consider the building's position as soon as possible.

Funding is prioritised by risk

Funding is prioritised according to cladding fire safety risk, with buildings presenting a high life-critical cladding fire safety risk being prioritised.

Can a Building Under 11 Metres Qualify for Funding?

Buildings under 11 metres are not automatically eligible simply because they contain cladding.

Funding may be available where a Fire Risk Appraisal of External Walls (FRAEW) undertaken by a suitably qualified and competent professional in accordance with PAS 9980:2022 identifies unsafe cladding presenting a serious life-critical fire safety risk. This FRAEW under 11 metres requirement is central to establishing eligibility.

The funding applies to eligible buildings containing two or more dwellings in England.

Homes England will review and audit FRAEWs submitted as part of the application process to determine whether the proposed remediation works are necessary and proportionate.

Meeting the eligibility requirements does not automatically create an entitlement to funding, and applications remain subject to prioritisation and available funding.

Who Can Apply?

Applications must be submitted by the building's Responsible Entity or their authorised representative.

This may include:

  • Freeholders
  • Head leaseholders
  • Right to Manage companies (RTMs)
  • Resident Management Companies (RMCs)
  • Registered providers of social housing
  • Other organisations with legal responsibility for repair and maintenance of the building

Individual leaseholders and residents cannot submit the formal funding application themselves.

What Is a FRAEW?

A Fire Risk Appraisal of External Walls (FRAEW) assesses the fire risk associated with the external wall construction of a building.

For this external wall funding under 11m route, the assessment should be undertaken by a suitably qualified and competent professional in accordance with PAS 9980:2022.

The FRAEW is an important part of establishing whether external wall remediation is necessary and proportionate.
