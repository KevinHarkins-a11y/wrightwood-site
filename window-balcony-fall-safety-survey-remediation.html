Remediation

Balcony & Guarding Remediation

Typical balcony and guarding remediation works may include:

Balustrade extensions
Additional vertical guarding
Infill panels
Closing excessive gaps
Replacement balustrade components
Local guarding repairs
Replacement of defective components

Straightforward repairs vs. structural work

Where significant structural alteration or full balustrade replacement is required, the works may require specialist design, structural assessment and project-specific technical review before installation. We identify where this applies and coordinate the appropriate specialists rather than proceeding without the necessary design input.

Regulatory context

Supporting Residential Fall-Safety Management

Our surveys and remedial programmes can support landlords in identifying and managing fall risks associated with windows, balconies and guarding in the context of the Housing Health and Safety Rating System (HHSRS), the Decent Homes Standard and relevant Building Regulations and guidance.

A survey and remediation programme is one part of a landlord's wider approach to managing fall risk. Installing a window restrictor or completing a specific remedial item does not, by itself, guarantee compliance with any particular standard — outcomes depend on the individual property, the assessment undertaken and the wider context of the building.

Wrightwood Consulting coordinates the survey, specification, supply, installation and sign-off of remedial works. We do not provide legal or regulatory certification of compliance.
